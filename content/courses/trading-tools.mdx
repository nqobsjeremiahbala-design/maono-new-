## What you will learn

The right tools and platforms are foundational to professional trading. This course walks you through setting up and using the tools every serious trader needs.

## Who this is for

Beginners and intermediate traders who want to ensure their trading infrastructure is properly set up.

## Course outline

### Module 1 — MetaTrader setup
Installing, configuring, and navigating MT4/MT5.

### Module 2 — TradingView
Using TradingView for charting, alerts, and analysis.

### Module 3 — The economic calendar
Understanding high-impact news events and how to trade around them.

### Module 4 — Risk management tools
Position sizing calculators and how to use them on every trade.

### Module 5 — Choosing a broker
What to look for in a regulated forex broker.
