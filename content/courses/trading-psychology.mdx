## What you will learn

Technical analysis alone will not make you profitable. This course addresses the mental side, the part most traders ignore until it is too late. Four short, focused lessons across two modules — read in under an hour, applied for the rest of your trading career.

## Who this is for

Any trader who knows what to do but struggles to execute their plan consistently.

## Course outline

### Module 1, The mindset problem
Why intelligent people consistently make irrational trading decisions, and the emotional states that drive overtrading.

- **Why psychology is the real edge** — strategy is what you know, psychology is what you do when you know it.
- **Why overtrading happens** — revenge, boredom, and FOMO, plus the rule-based fix that beats willpower.

### Module 2, Building the habit
Practical routines and rituals that turn discipline into a default rather than a daily struggle.

- **The pre-trade ritual** — three questions that catch most bad trades before they happen.
- **The Sunday review** — ten minutes a week that compound into consistency.
