## What you will learn

Technical analysis alone will not make you profitable. This course addresses the mental side, the part most traders ignore until it is too late.

## Who this is for

Any trader who knows what to do but struggles to execute their plan consistently.

## Course outline

### Module 1, The psychology problem
Why intelligent people consistently make irrational trading decisions.

### Module 2, Emotional triggers
Identifying the emotions that cause you to break your rules.

### Module 3, Process over outcome
How to evaluate your trading on process, not P&L.

### Module 4, Building consistency
Routines, habits, and structures that support disciplined trading.

### Module 5, The trading journal
How to use journaling to accelerate improvement and spot patterns.
