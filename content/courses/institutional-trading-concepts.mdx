## What you will learn

The majority of retail traders lose because they trade against institutional order flow. This course teaches you to identify where institutions are positioned and how to align your trades with smart money.

## Who this is for

Advanced traders who understand price action and want to add institutional context to their analysis.

## Course outline

### Module 1, How markets really work
The role of market makers, liquidity providers, and institutional order desks.

### Module 2, Liquidity and stop hunts
How institutions engineer liquidity to fill large orders.

### Module 3, Order blocks
Identifying institutional order blocks and using them as high-probability zones.

### Module 4, Fair value gaps
Understanding imbalances and how price seeks to fill them.

### Module 5, Market maker models
The intraday and daily frameworks institutions use to deliver price.

### Module 6, Live application
Applying all concepts to real market analysis and trade planning.
