## What you will learn

A profitable strategy is not just about entry signals — it is a complete system with defined rules for entry, exit, risk, and trade management. This course teaches you to build one.

## Who this is for

Intermediate traders who want to move from random trading to a rule-based system they can test and trust.

## Course outline

### Module 1 — Strategy fundamentals
What separates a strategy from a method. Edge, expectancy, and risk-reward.

### Module 2 — Trend following
How to identify and trade with the dominant trend.

### Module 3 — Range and consolidation
Trading between defined levels when markets are not trending.

### Module 4 — Breakout trading
Identifying and trading breakouts from consolidation zones.

### Module 5 — Backtesting
How to test your strategy on historical data to validate edge.

### Module 6 — Strategy optimisation
Adapting your strategy to your available trading time and risk tolerance.
