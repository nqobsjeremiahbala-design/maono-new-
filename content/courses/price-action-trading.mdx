## What you will learn

Master the art of reading price charts without relying on lagging indicators. This course teaches you to see what institutions see — pure price structure.

## Who this is for

Intermediate traders who have trading basics down but are struggling with consistency due to indicator dependency.

## Course outline

### Module 1 — The case against indicators
Why most retail traders lose using indicators and what to use instead.

### Module 2 — Support and resistance
Identifying key levels where price is likely to react.

### Module 3 — Candlestick patterns
The patterns that actually matter and how to read intent from candles.

### Module 4 — Market structure
Understanding trend, consolidation, and reversal through price structure.

### Module 5 — Entry techniques
How to enter high-probability trades with tight risk.

### Module 6 — Trade management
How to manage open positions and take profits without second-guessing.
