This is an example of how we prepare for a trading week inside the community. No live signals, no predictions — just the process we go through on a Sunday evening before Monday opens.

## Step 1: Context on the weekly

First question: **is price in trend, or in range, on the weekly chart?**

On EURUSD right now, we are in a multi-month range between roughly 1.0600 and 1.1100. That matters because range behaviour is different from trend behaviour. In a range, the edges are where the trades are. In a trend, pullbacks are where the trades are.

If you do not know which mode the market is in, you will fight the wrong battle all week.

## Step 2: Mark the levels on the daily

Three levels we care about this week:

- **1.0850** — mid-range, reacted four times in the last six weeks. Decision point.
- **1.0940** — previous week's high, and a 4-hour supply zone.
- **1.0760** — previous month's low and a clean demand zone.

That is it. Three levels, three reasons. Anything else is noise until price gets near one of them.

## Step 3: Plan the reactions, not the direction

We do not predict where EURUSD will go this week. We plan what we will do **if** it reaches a level.

- If price pushes into 1.0940 and shows rejection on the 4-hour, we are looking for shorts back toward 1.0850.
- If price breaks 1.0940 with momentum and retests it cleanly, we flip long, targeting 1.1020.
- If price breaks 1.0760 to the downside, we stand aside and wait for the next level, because the range is invalidated and we do not yet know what the new context is.

## Step 4: Know when not to trade

Big data events this week: CPI on Wednesday, ECB on Thursday. Our default around those is to close open trades thirty minutes before release and not re-enter for at least an hour after. News days create unreliable reactions at levels. That is not where our edge is.

## Why we share this

The point of a weekly breakdown is not to be right about direction. It is to get to Friday with a clean set of notes about what actually happened versus what we expected, so the Sunday review the next week is meaningful.

Over a year, the pattern in your notes is your edge. Before you can study the pattern, you have to keep the notes.

## Try it this week

Pick one pair. Spend twenty minutes on Sunday evening doing steps one to four above. On Friday night, review. Even if you never take a trade, you will understand the pair better than 90% of retail traders do.
