## The forex market in plain English

Forex (foreign exchange) is the global marketplace where currencies are bought and sold. It is the largest financial market in the world, over $7 trillion trades hands every single day.

Unlike stock markets, forex has no central exchange. It operates 24 hours a day, five days a week, across major financial centres in Sydney, Tokyo, London, and New York.

## Who trades forex?

- **Central banks**, Setting monetary policy, defending currency pegs, managing reserves
- **Commercial banks**, Facilitating international trade and corporate hedging
- **Hedge funds and institutional traders**, Speculation, arbitrage, and macro positioning
- **Retail traders**, Individuals like you, trading through brokers

## Why do most retail traders lose?

Retail traders represent a tiny fraction of daily volume. When you place a buy order, you are trading against banks, funds, and algorithms with far more information, capital, and infrastructure than you have.

Most retail strategies, based on lagging indicators like RSI, MACD, and moving averages, generate signals after institutions have already moved price.
