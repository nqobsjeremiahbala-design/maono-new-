Support and resistance is the most misused concept in trading. Most charts you see online have a level drawn on every second swing. That is not support and resistance. That is clutter.

Here is a one-page version of how to do it properly.

## Start from the top down

Always start on the **higher timeframe**. Daily first. Then 4-hour. Only then look at the 1-hour.

On the daily, mark no more than five levels on any pair. If you have more than five, you are marking noise.

## What makes a level real

A valid level has three things:

- **Multiple touches** — price has respected it more than once.
- **A clean reaction** — the reaction was fast and directional, not sideways chop.
- **Recency** — it has mattered in the last three to six months. A 2019 level on EURUSD is not your concern.

If a level does not have all three, skip it.

## Draw with zones, not lines

Levels are not pixels wide. Draw a zone — the wick on one side, the body close on the other. Price reacts inside the zone, not exactly on a line.

A zone that is too wide is useless. Keep it to the extremes of the meaningful candles in the area.

## Ignore these "levels"

- **Micro levels on the 5-minute** — they exist, but they are not tradable on their own.
- **Round numbers** for the sake of round numbers — 1.1000 only matters if price has reacted there.
- **Fibonacci retracement levels** — useful as context, not as "levels" in the support/resistance sense.
- **Trendlines drawn from two points** — one more touch does not make a trendline. You need at least three.

## What to do when price reaches a level

Do not trade the level. Trade the **reaction** at the level.

A clean pin bar or engulfing candle rejecting a daily support is a signal. A break and retest of a 4-hour resistance is a signal. Price simply arriving at a level is not.

## Weekly ritual

Every Sunday, re-mark your charts on the daily and 4-hour. If your levels from last week still hold up, keep them. If they were invalidated, delete them and draw new ones.

Your chart should get cleaner over time, not busier.
