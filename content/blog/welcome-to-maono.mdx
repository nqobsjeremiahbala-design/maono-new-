## Why we built Maono

The South African forex education space is flooded with promises. Lamborghinis, R50k months, passive income, all built on retail indicator strategies that fail 90% of traders.

We built Maono because we believe traders deserve better: honest education, institutional concepts, and a real community.

## What we teach

We teach institutional concepts, how banks, hedge funds, and professional traders actually read the market. Not indicators. Not guesswork. Price structure, liquidity, and order flow.

## Who we are

A small team of South African traders with 7+ years of live market experience. We have been through the losses, the psychology battles, and the breakthroughs. We teach what we know works.

## What to expect from us

No overnight wealth promises. No fake urgency. No Lamborghinis.

Just honest, structured education, and a community of traders growing together.

Welcome to Maono.
