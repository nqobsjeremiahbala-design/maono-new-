The most boring rule in trading is also the one that keeps accounts alive: never risk more than 1 percent of your account on a single trade.

Most new traders hear this, nod, and then promptly ignore it. It sounds too slow. "I need to grow my account. 1 percent is nothing."

That reaction is exactly why most new accounts do not survive the first year.

## What 1 percent actually means

On a R10,000 account, 1 percent is R100. That is your maximum loss on a trade. If the trade goes against you, you lose R100 and you move on.

The 1 percent is not the size of the position. It is the size of the loss if your stop gets hit. A R100 risk with a 50-pip stop means a completely different position size than a R100 risk with a 10-pip stop. Most trading platforms have a position size calculator for exactly this reason.

## Why the rule exists

The rule is not about being cautious. It is about being **unkillable**.

If you risk 1 percent per trade, ten losses in a row puts you down roughly 9.5 percent. Uncomfortable, but survivable. You can recover.

If you risk 5 percent per trade, ten losses in a row puts you down 40 percent. You now need a 67 percent return just to get back to even. Your judgement is compromised. Your confidence is shot. You are the trader who starts revenge trading.

**Professional traders do not risk small because they are timid. They risk small because they know variance is real, and even a great strategy has losing streaks.**

## The hidden benefit

There is a psychological benefit to the rule that no one talks about.

When 1 percent is at risk, you can be wrong without panicking. You can let the trade play out. You can follow your plan. You do not move your stop loss. You do not close early in fear.

The trader who risks 5 percent is managing an emotion. The trader who risks 1 percent is managing a process.

## What to do with this

If you are currently risking more than 1 percent per trade, the fix is immediate: calculate 1 percent of your account, calculate your stop distance, and use a lot size that matches. That is it.

You will not get rich faster by risking more. You will just get stopped out earlier.

The point of trading is not to win the next trade. It is to still be trading in five years. The 1 percent rule is how you get there.
