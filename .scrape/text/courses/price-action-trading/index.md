In this module we will show you how to trade without trading indicators, by using Price Action. Price Action Trading uses extremely simple, beginner level concepts and risk management to provide you with great trading opportunities without thousands of indicators or complex analysis 

What You Will Learn 

In-depth knowledge of candlestick patterns. 

Discern strong candlestick patterns from weak ones. 

How to draw trendlines and structures. 

How to identify Support and Resistance. 

Have a full understanding of how Price Action works. 

Read different types of Forex charts. 

Analyze price movements in a logical way. 

Understand why price moves in a particular way. 

Recognize key Supply and Demand levels. 

Understand how to use Japanese Candlestick Charts to improve trading decisions. 

Develop your own trading strategy. 

Requirements 

Basic understanding of Forex or Stock Market Trading. 

A computer or laptop. 

Desire to learn and achieve your goals. 

Trading View Account or MT4/5 platforms.
