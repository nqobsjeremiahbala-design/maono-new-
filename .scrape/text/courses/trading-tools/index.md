In this module we will fully focus on the key Trading tools. We consider this module as a complete Fibonacci trading package which is designed to provide you with profound information on how to fully utilize Fibonacci tools. 

In this module you will learn how to quickly determine exit & entry points for your trades and how to detect trend reversals and pull backs by using Fibonacci golden ratios and key support & resistance levels. 

What you are going to learn in this course can be used for trading securities in any freely traded market around the world. These include stocks, options, forex, cryptocurrencies, ETFs, and even bonds. Take your time to master all these concepts. 

What You Will Learn 

FREE TradingView account for chart analysis or MT4/5 platform. 

Enthusiasm for learning. 

Do not skip any lessons (All lessons are important). 

No Fundamental or Technical Knowledge required to take this course. 

Everyone can Learn to Become a Professional Trader. 

Requirements 

Learn all Fibonacci trading secrets and techniques to consistently beat the market with real chart examples included. 

How to build a strong foundation for Fibonacci trading. 

How to master Fibonacci & technical analysis basics. 

How to use Support & Resistance levels the right way. 

How to use Fibonacci Retracement and golden zones correctly. 

How to trade Fibonacci Retracements 

How to Trade Fibonacci Extensions. 

How to trade Fibonacci with Support and Resistance. 

How and where to place Stop Loss and Take Profit.
