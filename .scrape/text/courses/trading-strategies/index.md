In this module we will review Breakout, Fibonnacci, ABCD and Elliot Wave strategies. All these strategies use pure Price Action techniques.  Fibonacci methodology will mainly be used to determine our entries, stops and take profit levels. Fibonacci is used as well to determine our Support and Resistance using calculated levels. We use it as confluence with what market structure and price action is telling us. It becomes an amazing tool that helps us to look for both precise entries as well as targets. 

What You Will Learn 

How to apply a consistent trend by following trading strategy. 

How to piece together various confluence factors of price action and Fibonacci. 

How to properly manage a trade, including logical placement of stops and profits targets. 

Intraday and swing trading in stock market. 

How to Trade Fibonacci with Double Tops and Bottoms. 

How to Master Fibonacci & Technical Analysis Basics. 

How to generate your own signals. 

Requirements 

Knowledge in: 

Fibonacci Tools. 

Support and Resistance
