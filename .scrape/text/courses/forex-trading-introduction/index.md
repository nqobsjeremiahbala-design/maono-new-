This module is focused on beginner traders. If you know nothing about Forex and you want trading fundamentals, then this is the course for you. We did our best to optimize our mentorship approach, so that you can get the best start in your trading career. 

This course will teach you everything you need to know to launch your trading career in no time. From choosing the right broker to opening your first trade. This course has all the information that you need to be able to succeed in the trading world. 

What You Will Learn 

Trading Introductory Terminology. 

Difference between Fundamental and Technical Analysis. 

Different types of Trading Charts. 

Bullish Candlestick Patterns. 

Bearish Candlestick Patterns. 

Continuous Candlestick Patterns. 

Requirements 

A willingness to learn. 

No previous trading knowledge.
