This module is a complete step-by-step guide on how to create a consistent monthly income. 

In this module we will teach you to trade like Institutional Traders / Market Makers not like Retail Traders. As a result, you will have high probability winning trades with great risk reward ratios. 

Whether you are a brand new investor, or experienced trader, this course will provide you with the tools necessary to reach your financial goals. 

What You Will Learn 

Define your risk on every trade, before you even enter the trade. 

Advanced and refined Smart Money Concepts. 

Identification of Order Blocks. 

Trading imbalance Order Blocks. 

Learn how to make money on trades if the market goes UP, DOWN or SIDEWAYS. 

Identification of Distribution and Accumulation. 

Understanding of full Market Price Cycle. 

Execute high probability trades using Money Flow. 

See live trade examples, with REAL MONEY.
