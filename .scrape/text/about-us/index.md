## Maono Forex Trading 

From humble beginnings, the company was born from the mind of visionary and founder, Jody Francis, who grew a passion and grasped an understanding for the foreign exchange market. Built on the backbone of transparency and integrity, Jody felt it necessary to educate new and existing traders with key fundamentals and analysis thus establishing Maono Forex Trading. 

Comprised of an excellent management team with more than 7 years of trading and mentoring experience, we have created an intensive online and live trading course to help you master trading concepts which are applicable to any trading instrument such as Currencies, Stocks, CFD’s and Synthetic Indices. 

Maono’s vision and mission is one of empowering individuals with the necessary fundamental and financial innovation that is relevant to the current market structure whilst building and fostering teamwork and relationships well into the future. 

What is so amazing about our online trading course is that it is self-paced and we have created a system where you can track your progress, continue where you ended the last time you logged in, unlimited one-on-one refresher webinars, training course certification after completion and above all being given access to one-on-one interactions with our trainers for any further assistance. Our visual and literature methods are educational blocks to enhance the learning process and simplifies mastering effective and profitable trading concepts in the shortest time possible. We also offer a live 3-day course for those who prefer face-to-face interaction with our professional traders. 

So what are you waiting for, start your journey to FINANCIAL FREEDOM with our trainers who are highly skilled traders, market makers, and business leaders. Each one is experienced in forex trading, active traders on their accounts and take tremendous pleasure in teaching, hosting, and passing on the skill and knowledge of forex trading.
